- 💬 Ask me about **Echorural is an e-commerce platform dedicated to connecting rural artisans and producers with a wider market. We believe in empowering rural communities by providing them with the tools and resources to showcase their unique products to a global audience. Our mission is to bridge the gap between rural and urban economies, fostering sustainable growth and preserving traditional crafts. Every purchase made through Echorural directly supports the livelihoods of these talented individuals and contributes to the preservation of rural heritage.**
